DAILYMOTION

Founded in France in 2005, Dailymotion is a video-sharing platform where viewers can discover and watch content online. It offers a place for both short clips and longer videos, on desktop and mobile. The service is available through its website and mobile apps, so viewers can access videos wherever they are.

Explore a wide range of videos, including news, sports, music and entertainment. Content is shared by media organizations, publishing partners and independent creators, making it easy to find different perspectives and discover new channels. Viewers can browse topics and follow the publishers and creators whose videos they enjoy.

Dailymotion also provides video distribution and advertising solutions for publishers and creators. Its video player can be integrated into partner websites, helping publishers share video content with their own audiences. Learn more about the platform's history and its business model.
